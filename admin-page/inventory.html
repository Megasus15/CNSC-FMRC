Stock Level Rules

Set the threshold that determines when available stock becomes Low Stock.

  • Good — available quantity is above the threshold.
  • Low Stock — available quantity is at or below the threshold.
  • Out of Stock — no stock remains, regardless of the threshold.
  • Fixed quantity — use a remaining-stock count. A threshold of 5 marks an item Low Stock at 5 units or fewer.
  • Percentage — use a share of the starting quantity. A threshold of 20% marks an item Low Stock at 20 units remaining from an initial 100, or 2 units remaining from an initial 10.
  • Rules apply in this order: variant, item, category, then default. Leave an override empty to inherit the next applicable rule.
