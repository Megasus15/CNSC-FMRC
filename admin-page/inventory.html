Stock Level Rules

Pick the point where an item is tagged Low Stock instead of Good.

  • Good — more stock left than your number.
  • Low Stock — stock left is the same as your number, or under it.
  • Out of Stock — nothing left. This one never changes.
  • Pieces — you type the count itself. Type 5 and an item is Low Stock at 5 left or fewer.
  • Percent — you type a share of the starting stock, so big items get a bigger number. Type 20 and an item that started at 100 is Low Stock at 20 left or fewer, while one that started at 10 is Low Stock at 2 left or fewer.
  • The closest rule wins: variant first, then item, then category, then the default below. Leave a box empty and it just follows the wider rule.
