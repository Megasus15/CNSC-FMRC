How delivery estimates and fees work

Preparation and transit exclude the nonworking days below. Dates use Philippine time. FMRC covers any difference between the customer's accepted shipping fee and the courier's booking cost. These settings do not book a courier.

Delivery areas & fees

Add the places FMRC can deliver to. Set one fee per order and the number of working days needed.

How to choose delivery areas

Match province and city names to customer addresses. Use commas between multiple cities or postal codes. Leave both optional fields blank for province-wide delivery. A matching area with postal codes is used before one with city names, followed by a province-wide area. Customers outside the listed areas can choose pickup.
