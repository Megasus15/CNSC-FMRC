ABOUT US

The Fabrication and Manufacturing Research Center (FMRC) is a state-of-the-art shared service facility that bridges creativity and technology. It empowers students, educators, researchers, and businesses to transform innovative ideas into practical and functional outputs.

FMRC serves as a Shared Service Facility of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and a Common Service Facility of University of Camarines Norte (UCN). It supports Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) by enhancing creativity, design, and business innovation while increasing productivity and efficiency.

OUR VISION

UCN FMRC as a leading manufacturing and fabrication laboratory in the Bicol Region.

OUR MISSION

UCN FMRC shall provide an avenue for creative thinking and artistic design/work among its clientele using advanced technology means.
