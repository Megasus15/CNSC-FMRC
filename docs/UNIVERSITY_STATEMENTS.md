# University Vision and Mission

Release the matching frontend assets and backend content limits through the existing commit → push → online deployment workflow. No migration, Composer dependency, or ZIP upload is required. Shared asset references use version `2026.1113.7`.

In either Admin or Staff, open **Website Management → About Us → About Us Section Text → University Vision and Mission**. This is the first group in Section Text. Enter the approved university name, heading, labels, and statements, then choose **Save All Changes** and confirm. The official statements ship empty; no sample statement is published automatically.

The customer section appears after FMRC's Mission and Vision cards and before Who We Support only when both university statements contain text. You can save one statement while preparing the other. Clearing either and saving hides the entire section. The statements use plain text and preserve paragraphs and the names entered by the editor.

After online deployment, verify save/readback through both portals and check About Us on phone and desktop after entering both statements. Local fixture/browser checks do not verify hosted deployment.
